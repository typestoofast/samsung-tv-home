# Brand marks

Apple TV, Nintendo Switch, and Steam SVGs come from Simple Icons under CC0.
The fill is white; the paths are unchanged. The licence and exact source URLs
are in `app/assets/`. The Nintendo Switch mark identifies the owner's Switch 2.

The full-color Stremio mark comes from Stremio's official
[brand repository](https://github.com/Stremio/stremio-brand), which provides
these assets for use in Stremio apps and integrations. Its paths and colors are
unchanged. This asset is not covered by this project's MIT licence.

Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. These marks
identify shortcuts; they do not imply affiliation or endorsement.
